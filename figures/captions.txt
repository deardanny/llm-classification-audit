Figure 1. Sensitivity of topic classification to input grouping. (a) Joint decision states for 150 complete response pairs from 152 selected residual rows. Off and On denote identical labels in both repetitions; Mixed denotes disagreement between repetitions. Rows show batch states and columns show singleton states. (b) On-topic counts were 11 and 12 in batch calls, and 98 and 103 in singleton calls (denominator 150 each). Connectors link matching repetition indices, not independent experimental units. The model, prompt and target text were held fixed. Two rows remained unavailable after bounded format retries and are excluded. These selected-cohort results measure classification sensitivity, not accuracy. No inferential error bars are shown for two repetitions.

Figure 2. Audit outcomes and controlled interventions. (a) Jev audit-policy outcomes for 12,531 reconstructed Claude baseline labels; support and contradiction are not correctness labels. (b) Paired classifications under a fresh original-prompt control and a relevance-separated prompt for 201 selected disagreements. Cells report counts. (c) Directional changes between a fresh original-input control and repaired evaluation inputs among 1,171 rows affected by the ALL dictionary collision. Eighteen responses changed from off-topic to on-topic and four in the reverse direction; 15 and four, respectively, reproduced in the follow-up paired calls. The other 1,149 labels were unchanged. Of the target rows, 23 had no change within their own 800-character model input. Follow-up repeats were outcome-selected; interventions may also affect neighboring batch responses. Cohorts overlap, and their changes cannot be added as verified corrections.
